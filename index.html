      Questions

      Are my files uploaded anywhere?

      No. Your files stay on your device until the receiver downloads them, and they travel directly between the two devices. If a network blocks direct connections, encrypted pieces pass through a relay on their way, but they can’t be read and nothing is kept.

      Why do I have to keep the page open?

      Because your device is the source. That’s what makes Beam Beam private, free and unlimited in size. Keep the tab open and the screen on until the receiver finishes. On a phone, stay in the app; on a laptop, you can use other tabs.

      How big can a file be?

      There’s no limit from Beam Beam. The receiver needs enough free storage. Large files are written to disk as they arrive, so they don’t fill up the phone’s memory.

      What happens when the link expires?

      Nobody new can open it, and the QR code stops working. Downloads already in progress are allowed to finish. You can add 15 minutes at any time before then, or stop sharing straight away.

      Will it work on my office or school network?

      Usually, yes. Beam Beam tries a direct connection first, then falls back to an encrypted relay that runs over standard secure web connections, including port 443. Transfers through the relay are slower than direct ones but still private.

      Is the transfer really encrypted?

      Yes. Direct transfers use WebRTC’s built-in DTLS encryption. Relay transfers and all connection messages are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key that only exists inside your link. Add a password for an extra lock.

      Can several people download from one link?

      Yes, up to 8 at the same time. Each person’s progress shows on your screen. Your upload speed is shared between them.

      Do I need to install anything?

      No. Sending and receiving both work in any modern browser on any device. The apps are there if you prefer your own window, a home-screen icon or sharing from other apps.
